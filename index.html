My approach

Beyond the model.

The connecting thread across projects is framing questions, testing assumptions and turning research into something useful.

1

Find the real question

Identify the decision, the constraints and the evidence that matters. Most problems arrive framed too broadly or too narrowly.

2

Stress-test the story

Build scenarios and distinguish strong evidence from uncertain assumptions. The goal is to know where the thesis breaks, not to prove it.

3

Make it useful

Deliver a recommendation, model, process or system others can act on. Analysis that stays in a spreadsheet is unfinished work.
